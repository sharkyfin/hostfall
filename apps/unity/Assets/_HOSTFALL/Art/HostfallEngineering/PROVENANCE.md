# Service Junction

Original HOSTFALL geometry, authored in Blender 4.5.16 LTS on 2026-11-14.
No third-party mesh, texture, image generator or paid service was used.

Reproducible source: `tools/blender/make-service-junction.py` at repository root.
Export: Blender MCP selected-object FBX, Unity MCP Blender Bridge, metres preserved.
Measured dimensions: 0.68 × 0.2355 × 1.0515 m; 784 vertices, 756 polygons,
three material slots. Unity uses explicit opaque URP materials and a box proxy.

The original default Blender scene was retained; asset preview lives in its own scene.
