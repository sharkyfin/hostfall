CRACK is an inspiration for physical presence, not an animation source. No assets
were extracted from it. Imported motions are retargeted onto the existing service
suit rather than replacing the player or adding a floating arm rig.

## Selection and authoring checks

The downloaded Standard ZIP is 15,904,933 bytes. License is retained beside the art.
The selected list and reproducible exporter are in QuaterniusMotion/PROVENANCE.md.
The first Blender export revealed static poses: imported layered actions require
their action slot to be assigned. After correction, hand samples vary across time
(Walk vertical range about 0.10 m; Jog about 0.38 m; Interact about 0.43 m).
A second orientation check caught double axis inversion; the final export has the
left foot on negative X and toe direction facing forward.

Actual Unity captures include idle, two walk phases, sprint, armed body, interaction
and each first-person weapon. Hands use authored grip sockets when armed. Camera
bob/sway is bounded and adjustable to zero. Imported animation never moves the
CharacterController. Jump_Start/Hit_Chest are exported options, not advertised as
fully integrated traversal/hit-reaction states.

## Performance approach

Follow the [Unity profiler guidance](https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/6000.6/manual/analysis/profiler/performance-profiler-traces): measure a repeatable window and distinguish CPU,
allocation and rendering. Main-thread counters here come from a graphics-enabled
batch Editor; they are not a standalone GPU benchmark or a claimed campaign FPS.
Static map clipping, transient impact creation, held-model recreation and per-label
polling were concrete costs addressed in this pass. Exact final measurements and
regression results are in docs/qa/MOTION_COMBAT_2026-11-15.md.
