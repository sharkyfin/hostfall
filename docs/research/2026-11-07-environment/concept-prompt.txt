Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original environment art direction study for HOSTFALL, a grounded single-player first-person techno-horror game set in the evacuated NORTHLINE corporate engineering facility.
Primary request: A single coherent three-panel cinematic environment concept sheet, wide landscape. Three equal vertical panels depict consecutive rooms in the same credible terrestrial facility, not outer space. Restrained, achievable indie-game geometry and PBR materials, high quality art direction without extreme rendering spectacle. No characters, monsters, weapons, HUD or logos. No copying any existing game or downloaded model.
Left panel: an abandoned modern security operations room, about 10x8 metres with 3.3m ceiling. Chalky warm off-white walls and acoustic ceiling, muted sage painted lower wall panels, grey linoleum or raised office floor, steel desks, contemporary flat-screen monitors with tiny subtle terminal lines, fabric swivel chairs, paper shift handover notes and one mug. Readable neutral overhead practical light, one warm amber desk lamp left on, a displaced chair suggests an abruptly interrupted shift. Far doorway frames a colder corridor. Mostly maintained, NOT a decrepit rusty bunker.
Middle panel: narrow service corridor of the same facility. Concrete upper walls, desaturated petrol-green service panels, organised exposed cable trays and blue-grey cooling pipes with a red valve. One wheeled olive maintenance cart and an open electrical access panel. Segmented pools of warm white practical illumination, one single amber repair light. Route remains legible; a dark recess beyond a bend suggests uncertainty, not a black empty frame.
Right panel: functioning server room, off-white and brushed aluminium rack frames, dark server inserts, enclosed cooling channels, pale service floor. Cold neutral-white ceiling task lights, limited cyan equipment indicators. One isolated amber maintenance bay. Foreground detail, middle-ground equipment and far doorway separated by value and light. Slight localized condensation near a cooling joint, no blanket fog.
Lighting/mood: real techno-horror through absence and a single wrong operational detail, readable medium values, localized deep shadows, natural material colour visible. Contrast warm human work area with cool automated machine area. No constant flashing, no neon cyberpunk nightclub, no everything-black material treatment, no purple-pink rainbow lighting, no bloody horror props.
Composition/framing: architectural eye-level 1.65m, 28mm lens per panel; strong framing and depth; all three rooms visually consistent. Thin warm-white gutters and discreet small uppercase panel labels at bottom only: "SOC", "SERVICE", "CORE". Top small text exactly "HOSTFALL / NORTHLINE". Bottom small caption exactly "ART DIRECTION STUDY — NOT IN-GAME".
