# ADR 0024 · Home evidence and engineering atmosphere

2026-11-20

## Context

The Home computer returns the operator to physical evidence before the final
descent. Earlier records were easy to miss because their paths were available
only through the shell or a journal description. The front menu also lacked a
view of the facility, and the early engineering route needed recognisable
equipment and local sound.

## Decision

Home Knowledge has five short chapters that appear with the systems they explain.
Earned archive records are readable there and through the existing virtual shell.
Reading uses the same saved document flag in both places. The optional journal
entries after VENTFLOW and PAYLOAD lead back to Home for those records. After R26,
mandatory Recovery still reads its six records and releases the shuttle. Known
retired guide IDs restore to the first chapter. The legacy catalog serves the
older campaign and save reader.

NORTHLINE fittings use original Blender meshes built at metre scale, with a
wall-mounted isolation manifold, a caged inspection lamp and a grounded return
pump. The pump's mono loop is local to the equipment and pauses with the world.
The engineering studies of Beira D informed the use of flanges, enamel, steel
cages and local work light. [Environment artist portfolio](https://dominiquebuttiens.artstation.com/projects/dyaVOJ?album_id=983376)

The opening menu uses a muted loop captured from the game scene, with a still
from the same footage during preparation. The video texture is released when
leaving the main screen. New Game contains a short introduction before it
starts the campaign.

## Consequences

Saved documents, device IDs and the Recovery prerequisites stay authoritative.
The styling and acoustic changes cover the service passage and energy center.
