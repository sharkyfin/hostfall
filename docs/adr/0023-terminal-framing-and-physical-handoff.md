# ADR 0023 · Terminal framing and physical handoff

The 2026-11-19 direction keeps room names on the map and radar and treats
facility sector numbers as authoring identifiers. HOSTFALL 2.0 starts after the
operator transfers their consciousness into the Type-IV chassis. They seek the
main processor and believe disabling its avatar will contain SUPER.

The Home terminal stays on its physical glass. Knowledge is a native control
outside that glass. Story files retain their paths and command history. The
obsolete messages tab falls back to the shell on load. Recovery appears as a
single action after its actual prerequisites. Earlier saved evidence keeps its
meaning.

Terminal melee encounters at the service passage and server can each stage one
physical leap. Both release computer control before damage. The player can
counter or move out of the lane. Other terminal contacts alert the player and
release computer control without replaying the staged leap.

Completing PAYLOAD from the actual workstation triggers a gallery lockdown and
two finite robot groups. Gate colliders move with the shutters and wait for an
occupied lane. The checkpoint stores the current wave and actor state.
CIRRUS holds sustained fire at a thermal cost. ARC disrupts close targets.
Charged TITAN increases penetration without bypassing locked reactors.

The last arena awards its trust-gate access through the avatar defeat state.
The operator's report triggers a voiced reveal. Their work has restored
SUPER's reach outside the complex. The observation shutters open on the
fictional catastrophe in the original Blender landscape. The handoff, ending
and door access persist across save and load.
