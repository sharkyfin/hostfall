# World and progression

Keep the connected authored facility and stable door IDs. Start new campaigns in the existing engineer shelter (Z -14), near the actual HOME-PC at Z -16.5. The shelter blast door remains accessible for return. SOC is the first technical office; its old WS-07 becomes VENTFLOW with a new station identity, without reusing HOME's key.

## Mandatory graph (physical connections constrain prerequisites)

1. Home visit → SOC accessible. VENTFLOW stages seal an authored vent group, energise SOC/corridor access, then unlock corridor/server passage. SOC ambush is warned and physically emerges from an open service duct.
2. Server NETWORK DEFENSE disrupts coordination and grants DATA/vault access.
3. Vault optional FRAGMENT DOCK gives supplies/shortcut; main power branch becomes reachable through a physical route rather than the old cartridge-script dependency.
4. Energy CIRCUIT BREAKER controls light/turret/door power and reaches the quantum relay.
5. SIGNAL SYNC opens a timed barrier with readable recovery; safe waiting position and no permanent closure trap.
6. Logistics PAYLOAD RUNNER operates real lift, conveyor and bridge toward robotics.
7. Security MINEFIELD matches the real trap-room floor and opens a safe branch toward the neuroarchive.
8. DIGITAL DETECTIVE reveals the off-by-one incident date and access key; CONTAINMENT at robotics ties the branches into central access.
9. Eight completed systems + collected logical clues unlock home RECOVERY PROTOCOL. Three stages open a home-to-core shortcut and final quarantine battle. Defeating Core Avatar triggers the reveal, not a fake terminal completion.

This is a dependency design, not proof that the current physical graph meets it. Author access bindings and verify outward/return routes with the CharacterController. Door ids/poses before changes are in scene-audit.json. Do not award old terminal objectives to bypass the new puzzles.

Optional stations: passage BUFFER RECALL, vault FRAGMENT DOCK, cloud QUORUM DISPATCH, core INTEGRITY AUDIT. Each has a unique task and a nonmandatory reward; none is a required recovery clue or a hidden softlock.

the shelter's south dock and the east Ring dock. Their shuttle carries the player
through the lower tunnel in either direction. On arrival, reach the core gate from
the Ring side. The two-way shuttle and core approach passed the targeted Recovery
test. Connected Play checks walk from the shelter through the operator office,
server, vault, energy and quantum chain to logistics and across its bridge into
security. The extended Play check reaches the neuroarchive and the RBT-01
catwalk, captures R26, uses the north field release to return home and rides
the new shuttle to the core gate. See
[Connected campaign QA](../qa/HOSTFALL2_CONNECTED_CAMPAIGN_2026-11-19.md).

The in-game **Задания** journal follows the same route. Its current briefing
names the station or world interaction and lists the completion criteria.
The logistics lockdown, safe key, shelter shuttle, core approach and evacuation
report are separate steps. Optional stations appear when their existing access
conditions are satisfied. The next plot turn stays hidden until the player
reaches it.
